About

I’m Krishna, a developer based in India. I grew up wanting to be an innovator, and the first computer I saw decided what I would build with. I studied BCA at P.P.K. College and MCA at the University of North Bengal, where I got into robotics, research and building tools for the university.

Since 2020 I’ve shipped something every year. Most of my work sits where mobile, web and backend meet. I’ve taken apps from zero to launch, designed systems for client platforms, run DevOps for 20+ services, and added AI features to healthcare and consumer products. I’ve been experimenting with AI tools since 2020, and today Claude Code, GitHub Copilot and Codex are part of how I build every day.

Outside work I build my own products: Face Smash, a multiplayer Android game, and astory.fun, an AI-assisted fiction platform. I also publish open source, including Gati, a TypeScript backend framework.
